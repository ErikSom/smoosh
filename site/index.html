Smoosh

Liquids and soft materials for Box2D 3.

Open testbedExamplesDocsGitHub

Paint water, rubber, sand and clay. Add sources, drains, rigid bodies and motors. Requires WebGPU.

  • Clay extrusion press — squeeze, form and fold coloured clay.
  • Colour mixer — stir three streams together.
  • Pumps + waterwheel — turn piston motion into liquid flow.
  • Six tumblers — rotating containers full of liquid and loose bodies.

More scenes and settings are in the testbed's Samples and Simulation tabs. Touch controls included.
